# Insights (mock data, 3 October to 6 November 2026)

This summary is drawn from the mock dataset only: 3,038 sessions over 35 days,
an average of 86.8 sessions per day. Every number below was computed from the
generated data, not guessed.

## Most used tools
AI Writer is the most used tool, with 883 sessions (29.1% of all sessions).
AI Image Generator is second with 661 sessions (21.8%). Together the two tools
account for just over half of all usage. AI Code Generator follows with 451
sessions (14.8%), then AI Chatbot with 412 (13.6%), Speech to Text with 332
(10.9%), and AI VoiceOver last with 299 (9.8%). Text and image generation
dominate, while voice features are the smallest share.

## Peak hours
Usage has two clear peaks. The midday block 10:00-13:00 holds 976 sessions
(32.1% of the total), with 12:00 the single busiest hour at 272 sessions.
The evening block 19:00-22:00 holds 839 sessions (27.6%), led by 21:00 with
250 sessions and 20:00 with 248. The night is almost empty: 02:00-06:00 holds
only 76 sessions (2.5%), and 04:00 is the quietest hour with 4 sessions.
The pattern matches a working-hours plus evening audience in PKT.

## Weekday vs weekend
Weekdays average 99.6 sessions per day (25 weekdays), while weekends average
54.8 sessions per day (10 weekend days). Weekdays run about 82% busier than
weekends. The tool is used mainly on work days, which fits a tool people use
for work content rather than leisure.

## Trend
Usage rises slightly across the 35 days. The first week averages 80.7 sessions
per day and the last week averages 94.9, a rise of 17.5%. The rise is gentle
and steady rather than a spike, so it reads as slow growth, not a one-off event.

## Caveat
This is mock data, generated with a fixed seed and invented weights. The
patterns above describe the generator's assumptions, not real aiTransform
users. No business decision should rest on these numbers. Before acting on
any of it, replace this dataset with real usage data and re-run the same
dashboard.
